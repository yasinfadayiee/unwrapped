How do I track my Instagram followers without logging in anywhere?

Export your Followers and following from Instagram, drop the ZIP here, and do it again a week or two later. Each export is saved as a private snapshot in your browser, and comparing two shows exactly who followed you and who left. Instagram itself doesn’t show who unfollowed you.

What you track

Your follower history, in one place.

  • Followers over time — a chart of every snapshot you’ve saved.
  • New followers — who followed you, and how many in the last 30 and 90 days.
  • Who unfollowed you — by name, between any two exports.
  • Your biggest years — follow dates go back to the day you joined.
  • Backups — save your history to a file and move it to another device.
How it works

One export now, one later.

  1. Download your dataInstagram: Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information → Followers and following, format JSON.
  2. Drop the ZIP hereYour first snapshot is saved in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. Repeat every week or twoEach new export is compared automatically. Counts and the chart are free; every name and your full history are part of Plus.
FAQ

Follower tracking, explained.

How can I track who follows and unfollows me on Instagram?

Download your followers from Instagram now and again in a week or two, and drop each ZIP here. Unwrapped compares the snapshots and lists who followed and who left.

Does it track in real time?

No. We never log in to your account, so tracking works from the exports you download. Most people repeat it every one or two weeks.

Where is my history stored?

Only in your browser. You can save a backup file at any time and restore it on another device.

Is it free?

Yes — the analysis, the counts and the growth chart are free. Plus shows every name and keeps your full history.
