How it works

Three steps, all on your device.

  1. Export your messagesIn Instagram: Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information → Create export → your profile → Export to device. Choose Messages, date range All time and format JSON. Pick a higher media quality if you want photos in your chats, then tap Start export.
  2. Download the ZIPInstagram e-mails you when the export is ready. It stays under Available downloads for 4 days.
  3. Drop it here, open “Message viewer”Your browser opens the archive locally, so it works even in airplane mode. Reading every chat is free. Searching all chats at once and saving chats are part of Plus.
Inside the export

Where your chats are in the ZIP.

Each conversation is a folder under your_instagram_activity/messages/inbox/, named after the other person plus a number. Inside is message_1.json. Long chats are split into message_2.json, message_3.json and so on. Photos and voice notes sit in subfolders next to them.

Opened in a text editor, the files are hard to read. Meta saves accented letters and emoji in a broken encoding, so “José 😀” shows up as “JosÃ©” followed by a string of symbols. The viewer repairs this, joins the split files back into one chat and sorts every message by time.

FAQ

Instagram messages, opened.

How do I read the messages in my Instagram data download?

Request the export in JSON format, then drop the whole ZIP on Unwrapped and open “Message viewer”. Every chat is listed and opens like a messenger. The viewer reads JSON exports only, so if you picked HTML, request the export again in JSON.

Can I recover deleted Instagram messages?

Your download contains the messages Instagram still has for your account. Messages you deleted or unsent are usually not included, so the viewer can’t bring those back — but chats you can no longer find in the app often are. More about deleted messages.

Are my messages uploaded anywhere?

No. The archive is opened and displayed inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and nobody — including us — can see your chats.

Why don’t I see photos?

Photos and videos are only in the download if you included media. Request the download again with media and a higher quality setting.

Does it work with Facebook Messenger?

Yes. Drop the ZIP you export from Facebook’s Accounts Center (with Messages, in JSON) and open Message viewer in the Facebook tab — your Messenger chats open the same way.

Is it free?

Yes — reading every conversation is free. Plus adds search across all chats and saving chats as text files.
