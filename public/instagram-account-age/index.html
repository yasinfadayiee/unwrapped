FAQ

Your Instagram birthday, answered.

How can I see when I created my Instagram account?

Instagram shows the month and year a profile joined under “About this account”. Your data download has the exact sign-up date — Unwrapped reads it and adds all your other milestones around it.

Why is my oldest follower date later than my join date?

Your export only lists people who follow you today. Followers who left or deleted their accounts are gone, so the oldest remaining one can be years after you joined.

Does it work for TikTok, X and Facebook?

Yes. Drop several exports at once and every platform lands on the same timeline.

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are read in your browser and never uploaded. Nobody is notified.
