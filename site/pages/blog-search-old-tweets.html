How to search your old tweets

Scrolling back through years of posts doesn’t work, and X’s own search often skips the old ones. Your X archive has every post you made, and you can search it in seconds. Here’s how.

The short answer

  1. Request your archive in X: More → Settings and privacy → Your account → Download an archive of your data.
  2. When X says it’s ready (usually within a day), download the ZIP.
  3. Drop it, unopened, on Search & clean up.
  4. Type any word. Every matching post, reply or repost appears with the words highlighted.

Why X’s own search isn’t enough

The search box on X is built to surface what’s popular and recent, not to be an index of your own history. Old posts can be hard to reach, and there’s no clean way to say “show me everything I posted in 2014”. Your archive doesn’t have that problem: it’s a complete copy of what your account holds.

What you can do once it’s searchable

  • Full-text search over every post, reply and repost, with matches highlighted.
  • Filter by year and type (posts, replies, reposts), and sort by date or by most liked.
  • Search your own words: an ex’s name, a former employer, a place, anything you’d rather not have found later.
  • Run a clean-up check that flags swearing, alcohol and drug references, and personal info such as phone numbers and e-mail addresses, in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, French and German.

The clean-up check is a starting point, not a verdict. Read each flagged post yourself before you decide.

Deleting the ones you don’t want

Unwrapped can’t delete anything for you, because that would need your password and it never asks for it. Each result opens on X with one click, and you delete it there yourself. It’s slower than a bulk tool, and your account stays safe.

When this is worth doing

  • Before a job search: a recruiter or a new boss can scroll back through your history. Our guide to a social media clean-up covers the rest of your accounts.
  • Before you start posting for a business or a client.
  • Whenever you just want to find that one tweet from years ago.

Everything is read in your browser, so your posts are never uploaded. Searching and the clean-up check are free; exporting the results as CSV is part of Plus.

The quick way: advanced search

  1. On X, search for from:yourusername until:2012-12-31, with the date set to the end of the year you joined.
  2. Open the Latest tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Narrow the date if there are too many results.

Search can miss tweets, and it doesn’t show replies or deleted tweets reliably.

FAQ

How can I search all my old tweets?

Request your X archive, then drop the ZIP on Search & clean up and type any word. The archive contains all of your posts, so nothing is missed.

Are replies and reposts included?

Yes. You can filter by posts, replies or reposts. The clean-up check only looks at your own words, not at reposts of other people.

Can I search someone else’s old tweets this way?

No. The archive is only for your own account.

Does searching delete or change anything?

No. It only reads the file on your device. Deleting is always a separate step you do on X.
