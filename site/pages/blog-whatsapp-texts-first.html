Who texts first?

Export one chat, and see who starts the conversation, who replies faster and who writes more.

The short answer

WhatsApp doesn’t show it, but every exported chat has the time of each message. From that you can count who sent the first message after each quiet gap.

Check a chat

  1. Open the chat → contact name → Export chat → Without media. See how to export a WhatsApp chat.
  2. Drop the file into the WhatsApp chat analyzer.
  3. Open Who cares more to see who starts conversations, reply times and message counts for both of you.

The chat is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Read it kindly

Some people just have more free time, or prefer calls. Look at the trend over months rather than one week, and use it to start a conversation, not to win an argument.

FAQ

Does the other person know I exported the chat?

No. Exporting a chat sends no notice.

Does it work with group chats?

Yes. Group chats can be exported and analyzed the same way.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Both export formats are supported.
