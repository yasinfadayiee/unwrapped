How it works

Three steps, one minute.

  1. Open the chat in WhatsAppTap the name (iPhone) or ⋮ → More (Android) → Export chat. “Without media” is enough and much faster.
  2. Save or send the file to yourselfYou get a .zip (iPhone) or .txt (Android). Save it to Files or your computer.
  3. Drop it hereThe chat is read in your browser. Nothing leaves your device — not even the names.
FAQ

WhatsApp chat stats, explained.

Is it safe to analyze my WhatsApp chat here?

Yes. The file is opened by your browser and never uploaded. You can even turn on airplane mode after the page loads.

Does it work with group chats?

Yes. Every participant gets their own stats; Pro shows a detailed card for each person.

iPhone or Android?

Both. Unwrapped understands both export formats, 12- and 24-hour clocks and day-first or month-first dates.

Does the other person know?

No. Exporting a chat sends no notification, and the analysis happens only on your device.

Is it free?

The chat overview is free. Pro adds full word and emoji rankings and a card for every person.
