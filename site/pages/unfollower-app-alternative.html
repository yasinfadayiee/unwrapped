The trade-off: live alerts vs. a safe account

Unwrapped doesn’t send a push notification the second someone unfollows — nothing safe can, because Instagram doesn’t offer that to third parties. Instead you download a fresh export every week or two, and Unwrapped shows exactly who left in between. You give up “live” alerts and keep your account, your password and your data.

Switching from an unfollower app

  1. Remove the old app from your connected apps and change your password.
  2. Download your Instagram data (step-by-step guide) and drop the ZIP on Unwrapped.
  3. Repeat in a week or two to see who unfollowed you.

FAQ

Is there an unfollower app that doesn’t need my password?

Yes. Unwrapped works only with the data file Instagram lets you download yourself, so it never asks for your login.

Why can’t Unwrapped show unfollowers in real time?

Real-time tracking requires logging in to your account or scraping it, which risks blocks and breaks the platform’s rules. Comparing official exports is slower but accurate and safe.

Can Instagram ban me for using Unwrapped?

No. Downloading your information is an official feature, and the file is read on your own device without touching your account.
