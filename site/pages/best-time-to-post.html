How it’s calculated

Real signals, not rules of thumb.

  • X (Twitter): real engagement — the likes and reposts each of your original posts earned, by the hour and weekday you posted.
  • Instagram & TikTok: audience activity — when people followed you and when you receive messages, the moments your audience is around.
  • Two-hour windows — your top three, never two from the same evening.
  • Your local time — the heatmap uses your device’s timezone.

FAQ

Posting times, answered.

What is the best time to post on Instagram?

It depends on your audience. Industry studies often point to weekday evenings, but those are averages across millions of accounts. Unwrapped finds your own peak windows from when your audience follows you and messages you.

What is the best time to post on X (Twitter)?

For X, Unwrapped uses the real engagement of your past posts — likes plus reposts, averaged by the hour and weekday you posted — and shows how much better your best window performs than your average.

Which timezone is used?

Your device’s local timezone. If your audience lives elsewhere, the heatmap still shows when they’re active, converted to your time.

How many posts do I need?

For engagement-based results, at least 30 original posts. For audience activity, at least 30 follows or messages with dates.

Is it free?

Best Time to Post is part of the Creator plan. You can try it with made-up data in the live demo first.
