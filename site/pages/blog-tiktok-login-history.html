How to check your TikTok login history and devices

Check which devices are signed in through the app, then use your data download to review the login history TikTok has kept.

Check devices in the app

To review devices signed in to your account, follow the same tip shown in Unwrapped: Settings → Security → Your devices — remove anything you don’t recognise.

Review the login history in your data download

Your TikTok data download contains your login history. Choose All data and JSON when requesting the export. The file is a record of what TikTok has stored for your account, rather than a live list of signed-in devices.

What Privacy X-ray shows

Open your ZIP in Unwrapped for TikTok and view Privacy X-ray. It shows logins recorded, the number of IP addresses, top devices ranked by recorded logins, off-TikTok activities and ad interest categories. What you can review depends on the records included in your export.

Unwrapped reads the file on your device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and you do not need to give Unwrapped your TikTok password.

If you don’t recognise a device

Remove the unfamiliar device in the TikTok app and change your password. A device or IP record alone does not tell you who used the account. Unwrapped displays exported records; it cannot determine whether an account was hacked.

FAQ

Can I see who logged into my TikTok?

No. You can review device and IP records as TikTok stores them, but those records do not identify another person or establish their exact location.

Does Unwrapped need my TikTok login?

No. You request the file from TikTok yourself and open it in Unwrapped. It is read on your device without uploading it.

Should I choose JSON or TXT?

Choose JSON for a structured export that tools can read reliably. Unwrapped also reads TikTok’s Login History.txt records, but JSON is the recommended choice.

How often should I check my devices?

Check periodically and whenever you notice account activity you do not recognise. Use the app to review current devices; a downloaded file reflects the records in that export.
