How long does the Instagram download take?

You asked Instagram for your data and… nothing yet? Here’s how long it normally takes, where the file turns up, and how to fix the usual problems.

How long it usually takes

Small requests — for example only Followers and following — are often ready in minutes. A full download with messages, photos and videos can take hours or a few days. Asking only for what you need is the fastest way.

Where to find it

  • Instagram e-mails you when it’s ready.
  • You can also check Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  • The download link expires after a few days. If it did, simply request it again.

When something goes wrong

  • The file has no followers in it: it was requested as HTML or PDF, or with only media. Request again in JSON format.
  • Several ZIP parts: that’s normal for large downloads — keep them all and open them together.
  • Can’t pick the file on your phone: you’re probably inside the Instagram browser. Tap ⋯ and choose Open in browser.
  • On iPhone, the ZIP is in the Files app → Downloads. Don’t unzip it.

The fastest request for unfollowers

  1. Choose Some of your information → Followers and following.
  2. Format JSON, date range All time.
  3. Drop the ZIP into Unwrapped as soon as it arrives — it’s read on your device, nothing is uploaded.

FAQ

How long does the Instagram data download take?

Small requests are often ready in minutes; large ones with media can take hours or a few days.

Why can’t I find my Instagram download?

Check your e-mail and Accounts Center → Download your information. The link expires after a few days — request it again if needed.

Should I choose HTML or JSON?

JSON. It contains the same data in a form tools like Unwrapped can read.
