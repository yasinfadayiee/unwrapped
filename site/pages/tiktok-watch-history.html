FAQ

TikTok history, answered.

How do I see my full TikTok watch history?

The app only shows recent watch history. Your data download contains the full list TikTok keeps, with the date and time of each video. Drop it here and open Video finder.

Can I search by the video’s title?

No — TikTok’s export contains links and times, not titles or captions. Narrow it down by the day and time you remember, then open the candidates.

How far back does it go?

As far as TikTok keeps it for your account — usually months. The oldest and newest dates are shown in the date picker.

Is it private?

Yes. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded, and TikTok is not notified.
