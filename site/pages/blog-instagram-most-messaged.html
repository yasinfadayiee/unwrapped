Who do I message most on Instagram?

Instagram does not show a ranking of who you message most. Your data download contains the messages you need to count your busiest conversations.

How to find your most active chats

  1. Request your Instagram data, select Messages and choose JSON format.
  2. Open the ZIP in the Instagram DM analyzer.
  3. Go to Activity → Your DM besties to see conversations ranked by message count.

What the numbers mean

The Messages tile shows total messages and the number of conversations in your export. Your DM besties ranks conversations by number of messages, using the conversation titles from the export. Group chats can appear too.

Sent vs received splits messages you sent from those you received. The hourly message chart shows your busiest hours. You can read chats locally in the Message viewer. The ranking counts messages; it does not read or judge their text. A high count does not prove a close friendship.

Free and Plus

The top 3 conversations are free; Plus shows the top 10. For reply times and “left on read”, see the DM analyzer: those reply statistics cover 1:1 chats only.

Your chats stay on your device

Your ZIP is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded, nobody is notified, and no Instagram login is needed.

FAQ

Can I see who I message most without a tool?

Instagram does not provide this ranking in the app. Its data download contains your messages, which you can count yourself.

Does the other person know?

No. Reading your own export on your device does not notify anyone.

Does it include group chats?

Group chats can appear in the message-count ranking. Reply times and “left on read” statistics cover 1:1 chats only.

How many conversations are free?

The top 3 conversations are free. Plus shows the top 10.

What should I download?

Select Messages in your Instagram data request and choose JSON format. Open the downloaded ZIP in the analyzer.
