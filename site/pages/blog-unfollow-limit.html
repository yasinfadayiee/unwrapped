Instagram unfollow limit per day.

Unfollowing hundreds of accounts in one sitting is the quickest way to an “action blocked” message. Here’s how to clean up safely.

Is there an official limit?

No. Instagram doesn’t publish follow or unfollow limits, and they vary by account age, activity and how fast you act. What’s consistent in user reports: speed matters more than the daily total. Dozens of unfollows in a few minutes look automated.

A conservative pace

  • New or rarely used accounts: stay around 20–50 unfollows a day.
  • Established, active accounts: up to about 100–150 a day is commonly reported as safe.
  • Spread it out: small batches with breaks, not one long session.
  • These are cautious estimates from user reports, not Instagram rules — if you see a warning, stop for 24–48 hours.

If you get “action blocked”

  1. Stop following and unfollowing for at least a day.
  2. Don’t use automation apps or share your password — that makes blocks longer.
  3. Resume slowly, at half your previous pace.

FAQ

How many people can I unfollow on Instagram per day?

There’s no official number. Around 100–150 spread over the day is commonly reported as safe for established accounts; new accounts should go much slower.

How long does an action block last?

Usually from a few hours to a couple of days. Repeated blocks last longer.

Do mass unfollow apps get you banned?

Apps that log in as you and unfollow automatically break Instagram’s rules and are the most common cause of blocks.
