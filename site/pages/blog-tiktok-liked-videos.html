Your TikTok likes, with dates and links.

Find a video you liked in your profile, or browse your likes by day and time from your TikTok data download.

In the TikTok app

Your liked videos are on your own TikTok profile in the Liked tab, marked with a heart icon.

Get your likes with dates and links

Your TikTok data download includes your likes and favourites, each with the date and a link to the video. Choose All data and JSON when requesting the download.

TikTok’s export has no titles or captions. Use the date and link to identify a video, rather than searching for words from its caption.

Open your liked videos in Unwrapped

  1. Download the ZIP when TikTok says it is ready, then drop it into the TikTok watch history viewer. The file is read in your browser on your device; nothing is uploaded.
  2. Open Video finder and choose Liked. The Favorites and Shared tabs show those lists separately.
  3. Filter by day and time of day, then open each video again in TikTok.

To see your likes per month, open Habits & binges and look at the Likes chart.

Browsing is free. Exporting a list as CSV is part of Plus.

FAQ

Where are my liked videos in TikTok?

Open your own profile and look for the Liked tab with the heart icon.

Can other people see my liked videos?

That depends on your TikTok privacy settings for liked videos. Check your current setting before assuming the list is private.

Why does a video link no longer open?

The video may have been removed or made private.

Should I choose JSON or TXT?

Choose JSON for use with Unwrapped. Unwrapped also reads TikTok’s TXT like lists, but JSON is the recommended format for the data download.

Is browsing my liked videos free?

Yes. Browsing in Unwrapped is free; exporting a list as CSV is part of Plus. Your file stays on your device.
