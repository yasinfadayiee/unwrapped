Find the friend requests you sent on Facebook.

Sent a friend request and forgot about it? Pending requests can be hard to find in Facebook. Your data download lets you review the ones recorded in your export, then decide which to keep.

Find the requests you sent

The Facebook download includes sent_friend_requests.json for requests you sent and received_friend_requests.json for requests other people sent you. Keep the direction clear when reviewing the lists.

Download your Facebook information

Follow the Facebook data download guide. Choose your Facebook profile and include your friends data in JSON format:

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Tap Download or transfer information and choose your Facebook profile.
  4. Choose Some of your information and tick Friends and followers.
  5. Choose Download to device.
  6. Set Date range: All time, Format: JSON and Media quality: Low.
  7. Tap Create files. Facebook notifies you when the file is ready.

See sent and received requests in Unwrapped

Download the ZIP when it is ready and drop it into Unwrapped for Facebook without unzipping it. Open Friend requests: sent requests still pending are shown separately from requests you received. Where the export includes a date, Unwrapped shows when you sent the request.

This is a snapshot of the information in your download, not a live connection to Facebook. A request may have been accepted or cancelled since the file was created. Check the profile before acting.

Cancel a request from the person’s profile

Find the person on Facebook and open their profile. Check that your sent request is still pending, then use Facebook’s option to cancel it. Repeat manually for any other requests you no longer want to keep. Reading a download in Unwrapped does not change your Facebook account.

Keep your friends data on your device

Unwrapped reads the ZIP in your browser on your device. The export is not uploaded, and you do not give Unwrapped your Facebook password or access to your account.

FAQ

Where can I see friend requests I sent on Facebook?

Request your Facebook data with Friends and followers included in JSON format, then open the ZIP in Unwrapped and choose Friend requests. Sent and received requests are listed separately.

Does the sent list show every request I ever sent?

Treat it as the pending requests recorded in that download, not a complete history of every request you have ever sent.

Can I see requests other people sent me?

Yes. Unwrapped also reads received_friend_requests.json and shows received requests separately in the Friend requests view.

How do I cancel an old request?

Open the person’s profile on Facebook, check whether your request is still pending and cancel it there. Unwrapped does not cancel requests just by reading the file.

Why is the list empty or out of date?

Check that you chose the correct Facebook profile and included friends data. An empty list can mean the file contains no pending requests; an older download will not reflect later changes. Request a fresh export to review newer data.
