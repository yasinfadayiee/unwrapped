Add a link to your Instagram or TikTok bio.

A clickable link in your bio is the easiest way to send followers to your shop, newsletter or latest video. Here’s exactly where to tap on each app.

Instagram

  1. Go to your profile and tap Edit profile.
  2. Tap Links → Add external link.
  3. Paste the URL, add a title and save. Instagram shows the first link in your bio; extra links appear when someone taps it.

TikTok

  1. Tap Edit profile.
  2. Look for the Website field and paste your link.
  3. Don’t see it? TikTok only shows the website field to some accounts — for example business accounts or accounts above a follower threshold.

YouTube

  1. In YouTube Studio open Customization → Basic info.
  2. Under Links, add your URLs — they appear on your channel page.

One link for everything

Each app gives you limited room. A free link-in-bio page like myunwrapped.com/@you puts all your links, socials and a big Telegram or WhatsApp button on one page — and shows which links get tapped. Use the same link on every platform.

FAQ

How many links can I add to my Instagram bio?

Instagram lets you add several links in Edit profile → Links. A link-in-bio page lets you share even more with one link.

Why can’t I add a link to my TikTok bio?

TikTok shows the website field only to some accounts, such as business accounts. Switching to a business account in settings often adds it.
