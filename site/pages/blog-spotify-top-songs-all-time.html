Your most played Spotify songs, of all time.

Which songs and artists have you spent the most time with? Your extended streaming history lets you look beyond a single year of Wrapped.

Why Wrapped doesn’t show the whole picture

Spotify Wrapped shows your top songs and artists for one year. The app and Wrapped don’t show an all-time ranking. To find your favourites across the years, use your extended streaming history.

Get your extended streaming history

  1. On spotify.com, go to Account → Privacy settings → Download your data.
  2. Tick Extended streaming history. It can take up to 30 days to arrive.
  3. Drop the ZIP into Spotify stats. It adds up every play, on your own device.

Choose extended streaming history for an all-time view: the account data download alone covers only about the last year. Unwrapped reads the ZIP in your browser, on your device. Your file is never uploaded.

What your all-time Spotify stats show

  • Top artists and Top songs: rankings across the history in your file, ordered by minutes actually listened, not play counts.
  • Your number one: your leading artist, with the minutes you listened and the number of plays.
  • Top podcasts: podcast listening shown separately from your songs and artists.
  • Wrapped, every year: your most played artist for each year in your file.

Free shows your top 5 artists and songs. Plus shows your top 50 artists and songs and every year’s number-one artist. The results reflect the listening history in the file you provide.

FAQ

Can I see my all-time top Spotify artists as well as songs?

Yes. Drop your extended streaming history into Spotify stats to see Top artists and Top songs across the history in your file. Both rankings use minutes listened.

Are the rankings based on minutes or plays?

Minutes actually listened. A song you often skip can have many plays but little listening time. The number-one artist insight shows both minutes and plays.

Why does my last-year file give different results?

The account data download only covers about the last year, so its rankings reflect that period. Request extended streaming history to compare your listening across the years.

Are podcasts mixed into my top songs?

No. Podcasts appear separately under Top podcasts, rather than in the Top songs ranking.

Do I need to log in to Spotify on Unwrapped?

No. Download your file from Spotify yourself, then drop it into Unwrapped. It is read on your device without connecting to your Spotify account or uploading your file.
