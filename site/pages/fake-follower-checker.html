FAQ

Fake followers, explained.

How do I check if my followers are fake?

Download your followers list from Instagram or TikTok (the official data export), then drop the ZIP on this page. Unwrapped screens every follower for username red flags and checks for sudden waves of new followers, then gives you an audience quality grade.

How accurate is the fake follower check?

It’s an estimate from public signals in your own export — username patterns, spam words and follow timing. Accounts with several red flags are listed as “likely”, accounts with one or two as “suspicious”. Some real people have unusual usernames, so check before removing anyone.

Is it free?

Yes — the quality grade and the number of likely and suspicious followers are free. The exact accounts, the reasons, the follow-spike chart and CSV export are part of the Creator plan.

Does it remove fake followers automatically?

No. We never log in to your account, so nothing happens without you. Each listed account has an “Open” button; remove it from your followers in the app yourself.

Do you need my password?

Never. Everything is computed from the file the platform gives you, inside your own browser.

Does it work for TikTok and Threads?

Yes — any export with follower usernames: Instagram, Threads and TikTok.
