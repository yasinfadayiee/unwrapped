Your full TikTok search history.

The search bar shows a handful of recent terms. TikTok keeps much more — every search, with the date — and you can see all of it.

What the app shows — and what it keeps

Tap search in TikTok and you’ll see a few recent terms. Your TikTok data download contains the full list TikTok stores, each search with its date.

What you can learn from it

  • your most-searched words and accounts,
  • your recent searches, newest first,
  • alongside your watch history and real screen time.

See it in a few minutes

  1. In TikTok: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data, choose All data, format JSON.
  2. Drop the ZIP into Unwrapped and open Searches.

The file is read in your browser — nothing is uploaded. To clear searches in TikTok itself, tap search, then Clear all.

FAQ

Can I see all my old TikTok searches?

Yes — your TikTok data download includes the searches TikTok keeps, with dates.

Does clearing searches in the app delete them from TikTok?

It clears the list in the app. What TikTok keeps on its side is shown in your data download.

Is it private?

Yes. The file is opened in your browser and never uploaded.
