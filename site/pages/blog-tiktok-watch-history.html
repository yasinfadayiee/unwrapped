See every video you watched.

The app keeps a short list. Your data download has the full history with dates and links.

In the app

  1. Go to your profile → menu (☰) → Settings and privacy.
  2. Open Activity center and tap Watch history.
  3. Scroll by date, or search for a creator.

The app only keeps recent months, and you can’t export or search the list well.

Full history, from your data download

Your TikTok data download includes your video browsing history: every video you watched, with the date, time and a link to the video. Pick JSON when you request it, so tools can read it.

Drop the file into Unwrapped to see your watch time by day, your most-watched hours and a searchable list of every video, on your own device.

Find one video you lost

If you remember roughly when you saw it, open the history at that day and check the links one by one. Our guide on finding a TikTok video you watched goes through it step by step.

FAQ

How far back does TikTok watch history go?

The app shows recent months only. Your data download covers a longer period, depending on what TikTok has kept for your account.

Can other people see my watch history?

No. Only you can see it, in the app and in your own data download.

Can I clear my TikTok watch history?

Yes. In Watch history, tap Select and delete videos, or turn the history off in the same screen.
