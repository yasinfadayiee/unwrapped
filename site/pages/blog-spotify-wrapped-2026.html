Spotify Wrapped 2026: don’t wait for December.

Wrapped is the one day a year Spotify shows you your own numbers. But the data behind it is yours all year. Here’s when Wrapped 2026 will probably land, and how to see your stats right now.

When does Spotify Wrapped 2026 come out?

Most likely on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. Spotify hasn’t announced the date yet. But every Wrapped since 2021 has come out on the Wednesday after US Thanksgiving, and in 2026 that’s 2 December.

What counts towards your Wrapped?

Wrapped counts your listening from 1 January up to a cutoff that Spotify doesn’t publish. For years people assumed 31 October, but in 2023 Spotify said Wrapped “is still counting past Oct. 31”. The usual estimate now is around mid-November, so what you play in late November and December most likely won’t make this year’s Wrapped. A few other things to know:

  • It ranks by how much you listened, so one song on repeat for a week can beat a favourite you play now and then.
  • Listening in a private session may not count, because Spotify leaves those plays out of your taste profile.
  • White noise, sleep sounds and kids’ songs played on your account count too. That’s why some Wrapped results feel off.

How to see your Spotify stats now

You don’t have to wait. Every Spotify user can download their own listening data, and it holds the same raw numbers Wrapped is built from:

  1. In a browser, go to spotify.com → Account → Account privacy.
  2. Under Download your data, tick Account data (about the last year, usually ready in around five days) and Extended streaming history (your whole history, up to 30 days).
  3. Confirm the e-mail, and when the ZIP arrives, drop it on Spotify stats.

You’ll see your top artists and songs by real minutes listened, when you listen by hour and weekday, your skip rate and your podcasts. Request it today and it should arrive well before Wrapped day, so you can compare your numbers with Spotify’s.

The full walkthrough is in how to get your Spotify extended streaming history.

See your Wrapped for past years

Spotify doesn’t keep old Wrapped stories around. Your extended streaming history goes back to the day you joined, though, so Unwrapped Pro can rebuild your number one artist and song for every year since you joined.

Why isn’t my Spotify Wrapped showing?

  • Update the app. Wrapped lives in the latest version of the Spotify app; older versions may not show it.
  • Look on the home screen or search for “Wrapped”. It doesn’t appear in the desktop app the same way.
  • Not enough listening. New accounts and accounts with very little listening may not get a Wrapped.
  • Kids’ and some managed accounts can behave differently.

If Wrapped never shows up, your data download still works, any day of the year.

FAQ

What date is Spotify Wrapped 2026?

Spotify hasn’t announced it yet. Every Wrapped from 2021 to 2025 came out on the Wednesday after US Thanksgiving (most recently 3 December 2025), so the most likely date is Wednesday 2 December 2026.

Can I see my Spotify Wrapped early?

Not Spotify’s own Wrapped, but you can see the same kind of stats today: request your Spotify data download and drop it on Unwrapped’s Spotify stats. Your top 5 artists and songs are free.

Does December listening count for Wrapped?

Probably not. Spotify doesn’t publish the cutoff. It said in 2023 that Wrapped counts past 31 October, and reports point to around mid-November.

Does Unwrapped need my Spotify password?

No. You request your data from Spotify yourself and drop the file in your browser. Unwrapped never connects to your account, and the file is never uploaded.
