How to find a TikTok you watched before

You saw a video, didn’t like it, and now you can’t find it. The app only shows recent history, but TikTok keeps more. Here’s how to get to it.

← BlogOct 19, 20263 min read

The short answer

  1. Request your data in TikTok: Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data. Choose All data and JSON.
  2. Download the ZIP when TikTok says it’s ready.
  3. Drop it, unopened, on the TikTok video finder.
  4. Pick the day and time of day you remember, and open the candidates in TikTok.

The full request steps are in How to download your TikTok data.

What the download has

  • Watch history: every video with the exact time you watched it, as far back as TikTok keeps it for your account, usually months.
  • Likes, favorites and shares, each in its own list.

Why you can’t search by title

The export contains links and times, not titles or captions. So you narrow it down by when you saw the video: the day, then morning, afternoon, evening or late night. Open the few videos around that time and you’ll usually spot it.

Tips to find it faster

  • Think about what you were doing: a commute, lunch, the night before an exam.
  • If you liked, saved or shared it, check those lists first. They’re much shorter.
  • If the video was deleted or made private by its creator, the link won’t open anymore even though it’s in your data.

The file is read in your browser and never uploaded, and TikTok isn’t notified. Browsing is free; exporting a list as CSV is part of Plus. The same file also gives you your real time on TikTok.

FAQ

How do I see my full TikTok watch history?

The app shows only recent history. Your data download contains the full list TikTok keeps, with the date and time of each video.

Can I search by the video’s title?

No. The export has links and times, not titles. Narrow it down by the day and time you remember.

How far back does it go?

As far as TikTok keeps it for your account, usually months.

Is it private?

Yes. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.
