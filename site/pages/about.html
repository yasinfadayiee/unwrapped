About

Your data, on your side.

Social platforms know a lot about you. Unwrapped helps you see it too — from the official data file every platform must give you, opened on your own device.

Who runs Unwrapped

Unwrapped is an independent product, built and run under the Unwrapped brand from the United Kingdom. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Instagram, Meta, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Spotify, Telegram or WhatsApp. Their names appear here only to say which data files Unwrapped can read.

Why we built it

People want simple answers: who stopped following me, who never follows back, how much time TikTok really takes, who leaves me on read. The usual way to get them is an “unfollower app” that asks for your password — risking your account and handing your data to strangers. We thought there had to be a safer way. There is: the data download every platform already offers.

What it reads

Unwrapped reads the official export files from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Spotify, Telegram and WhatsApp. A result can only be as complete as the file the platform gives you. When something is missing from your export, we say so instead of guessing.

How it works

  • No login, ever. You download your data from the platform yourself and drop the file here.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The analysis runs inside your browser. Our servers never receive your export — you can turn on airplane mode and it still works.
  • Your history stays with you. Snapshots are saved in your browser, and you can download a backup file at any time.

Check it yourself

You don’t have to take our word for it:

  • Watch the network. Open your browser’s developer tools, go to the Network tab, then drop your file. You will not see your export being sent anywhere. The only requests are tiny anonymous counters (under 1 KB, no cookies, no IP stored), such as “one analysis was run today”.
  • Go offline. Load the page, switch on airplane mode and drop your file. The analysis still runs.
  • Look for a password field. There isn’t one. Unwrapped never asks you to sign in to a social account.

How we make money

No ads, no data sales, no tracking pixels. The core analysis is free; paid plans unlock full lists, deeper tools and reports. You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Telegram Stars, or in crypto. There is no auto-renewal: a plan simply ends, and you decide if and when to extend it. See Pricing.

What we promise

  • We will never ask for your social media password.
  • We will never upload, store or sell your export.
  • We will tell you plainly what a result can and can’t prove.
