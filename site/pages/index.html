07 — Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is it really private?

Yes. Your export is opened and analyzed entirely inside your browser — it is never uploaded, and our servers never see it. Check it yourself: open your browser’s developer tools (Network tab) or turn on airplane mode after the page loads. The analysis still works.

The only time this site talks to its own server is for licenses and payments — never with your social data.

Why don’t you ask for my password like other apps?

Because giving your password to a third-party app is the most common way accounts get hacked, restricted or banned — and the platforms forbid it. The official data export contains the same information, with none of that risk.

Can one export show who unfollowed me?

One export is a snapshot: it shows who doesn’t follow you back, your fans, pending requests and much more. Drop a newer export later and Unwrapped compares the two automatically — that’s how you see exactly who unfollowed you, by name.

How long does the export take?

Instagram and Facebook: usually minutes, at most about 48 hours. TikTok: a few hours to a few days. X: around 24 hours. LinkedIn: about 10 minutes for connections.

Which file do I upload?

The ZIP the platform sends you, exactly as it is. Split into several ZIP files? Select them all at once. Already unzipped? Choose the folder. JSON, HTML and TikTok’s TXT format all work.

Will this get my account banned?

No — we never touch your account. You download your own data with the platform’s official tool. The unfollow queue only opens profiles for you; you tap unfollow yourself, at a pace platforms are comfortable with.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Download the ZIP on your phone, open this site and tap “Choose my export”. It works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android.

What is stored, and where?

Only snapshots of follower lists, inside your own browser (IndexedDB), so we can tell you who unfollowed you next time. Delete them any time from My license → Local data.
