Who doesn’t follow you back on X?

X stopped most third-party follower apps when it closed its free API. Your own X archive still contains both lists — and that’s all you need.

Why most X unfollower apps stopped working

Follower-tracking apps used to read your lists through X’s API. Since API access became paid and restricted, many apps shut down or ask for full account access. Your data archive is the reliable route that’s left.

Check it in a few minutes

  1. On X: Settings and privacy → Your account → Download an archive of your data. X prepares it within about a day.
  2. Drop the archive ZIP on Unwrapped. It’s read in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
  3. Open Not following back. The archive lists accounts by their ID, so every row has a button that opens the profile on X.

What else your X archive shows

  • Your old tweets, searchable, with a clean-up check for risky posts.
  • Your best posts ever and your posting habits.
  • The interests and advertisers X has attached to you, and connected apps.

FAQ

Can I see who unfollowed me on X?

Yes, by comparing two archives from different dates. Unwrapped saves each one as a private snapshot and compares them.

Why does the archive show IDs instead of usernames?

That’s how X stores your follower lists. Each ID opens the right profile, even if the person changed their @name.

Is it free?

Yes, the analysis is free. Plus shows the full lists and keeps your history.
