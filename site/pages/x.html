X FAQ

X archives, explained.

How do I view my Twitter / X archive?

Request it in Settings and privacy → Your account → Download an archive of your data. When it’s ready, download the ZIP and drop it here — no unzipping needed.

Can I search my old tweets and find ones to delete?

Yes. Open Search & clean up to search every post, reply and repost by word and year. The clean-up check flags swearing, party posts and personal info, and each post opens on X with one click so you can delete it. More about it.

Why do followers show as account numbers?

X’s archive lists followers by account ID, not by username. Tap “Open” next to any entry and X shows you who it is.

My archive is several gigabytes. Will it work?

Yes. Unwrapped reads only the small data files inside the ZIP and skips your photos and videos, so even huge archives open in seconds.

Can I see who unfollowed me on X?

Yes, by comparing a newer archive with an older one you analyzed before — the snapshot is kept privately in your browser.
