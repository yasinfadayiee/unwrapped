How it works

Three steps, one minute.

  1. Open Telegram DesktopOn your computer: open the chat → ⋮ → Export chat history. For all chats: Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data.
  2. Choose JSONUntick photos and videos and set the format to Machine-readable JSON. You get a folder with result.json.
  3. Drop it hereThe file is read in your browser. Nothing leaves your device — not even the names.

FAQ

Telegram chat stats, explained.

Is it safe to analyze my Telegram chat here?

Yes. The file is opened by your browser and never uploaded. You can even turn on airplane mode after the page loads.

Does it work with group chats?

Yes. Every participant gets their own stats; Pro shows a detailed card for each person.

Does the other person know?

No. Exporting a chat sends no notification, and the analysis happens only on your device.

Is it free?

The chat overview is free. Pro adds full word and emoji rankings and a card for every person.
