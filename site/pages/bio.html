FAQ

Link in bio, explained.

What is a link in bio page?

Instagram and TikTok let you add one link to your profile. A link in bio page turns that single link into a page with all your links — shop, videos, contact, socials.

Is Unwrapped Bio free?

Yes. The free page includes up to 5 links, your socials, six themes and total views. Any Unwrapped plan unlocks Pro: up to 100 links, unlimited contacts, per-link click analytics and no “Made with” badge.

Do I need to connect my Instagram account?

No. You never give us a password or connect any account — you simply paste your page link into your bio.

How do I edit my page later?

Your browser remembers it. Add an e-mail and we send you a private edit link, so you can edit from any device.
