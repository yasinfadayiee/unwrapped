FAQ

Old tweets, sorted.

How can I search all my old tweets?

X’s own search often misses old posts. Your archive contains all of them. Drop it here, open “Search & clean up” and type any word — results appear instantly, with the matches highlighted.

Can Unwrapped delete my tweets?

No — that would need your password, and we never ask for it. Each result opens on X with one click, where you delete it yourself. Your account stays safe.

What does the clean-up check look for?

Swearing, alcohol and drug references, and personal info such as phone numbers and e-mail addresses — in four languages. Add your own words for anything else. It’s a starting point, not a verdict: read each post yourself.

Are replies and reposts included?

Yes. Filter by posts, replies or reposts. The clean-up check counts only your own words, not reposts of other people.
