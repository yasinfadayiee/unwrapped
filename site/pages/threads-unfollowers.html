FAQ

Threads unfollowers, explained.

Can I see who unfollowed me on Threads?

Threads doesn’t show it in the app. Comparing two of your own Threads data downloads shows exactly who left.

Are Threads unfollower apps safe?

Apps that ask for your Instagram or Threads password put your account at risk. Unwrapped only reads the file Threads gives you.

Does the person know I checked?

No. You only read your own downloaded data.

Is it free?

Yes — the analysis and the counts are free. Plus shows every name and keeps your full history.
