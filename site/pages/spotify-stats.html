FAQ

Spotify stats, explained.

Can I see my Spotify Wrapped before December?

Yes. Your own data download has the same listening history, so you can see your top artists and songs whenever you like.

Do I need to log in with Spotify?

No. Unwrapped never connects to your account — it only reads the file Spotify e-mails you.

Account data or extended streaming history?

Account data covers the last year and arrives faster. The extended history covers your whole account and unlocks the yearly Wrapped.

Is it free?

The overview with your top 5 is free. Pro shows your top 50, every year’s Wrapped and new discoveries.
