Instagram Wrapped: your year, unwrapped.

Spotify has Wrapped. Instagram doesn’t — but your data download holds everything needed for one: who you talk to, what you like and when you’re online.

What goes into your Wrapped

  • your top people — who you message, like and comment on most,
  • your busiest months and hours,
  • totals for likes, comments, messages and posts,
  • follower changes and your account age.

It works for TikTok, X, Facebook and LinkedIn exports too — and you can combine platforms.

Make yours in a few minutes

  1. Download your Instagram data (format JSON, all time).
  2. Drop the ZIP into Unwrapped and open Wrapped cards.
  3. Pick a theme and share the cards to your story.

Private by design

Your file is read in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and only the cards you choose to share leave your phone. Try it first with the live demo.

FAQ

Does Instagram have a Wrapped?

No official yearly Wrapped like Spotify. Unwrapped builds one from the data file Instagram gives you.

Is it free?

Yes. Free cards have a small watermark; paid plans remove it and unlock every theme.

Do I need to log in?

No. You never give your password — you only open your own downloaded file.
