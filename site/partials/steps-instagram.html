Request your export

  1. In the Instagram app: your profile → ☰ menu → Accounts Center.
  2. Your information and permissions → Export your information → Create export.
  3. Pick your Instagram account → Export to device.
Open the download page

Choose these options

  1. Customize information → tick Followers and following · date range All time.
  2. Format JSON (HTML works too) · media quality Low.
  3. Want DM stats and the full privacy X-ray? Choose All available information instead.

Drop the ZIP here

Instagram e-mails you when the file is ready — usually within minutes, sometimes up to 48 hours. Download the ZIP and drop it on this page. That’s it.
