Three steps, about three minutes.

FAQ

Exporting followers, explained.

How do I export my Instagram followers to Excel?

Download your followers and following from Instagram (JSON), drop the ZIP on this page, open the list you want and tap CSV. The file opens directly in Excel or Google Sheets.

Can I export someone else’s followers?

No. Instagram only gives you your own data, and we never scrape other accounts.

Does the export include follow dates?

Yes — the date each follow happened is in your Instagram data and is included in the CSV.

Is it safe?

Yes. You never give us your password and the file never leaves your device.
