Facebook FAQ

Unfriends, explained.

How can I see who unfriended me on Facebook?

Download your Facebook information (Friends and followers, JSON) and drop it here — that becomes your first snapshot. Repeat in a few weeks: Unwrapped compares both and lists everyone who disappeared, excluding people you removed yourself.

Will the person know I checked?

No. Nobody is notified — you are only reading your own data download, on your own device.

Why might someone appear as unfriended by mistake?

Deactivated or deleted accounts also vanish from friend lists, and people who change their display name can look like a new person. Check the profile before drawing conclusions.
