LinkedIn FAQ

LinkedIn exports, explained.

How do I export my LinkedIn connections?

Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → choose Connections → Request archive. LinkedIn e-mails you a download link, usually within about ten minutes.

Why are most e-mail addresses missing?

LinkedIn only includes the e-mail of connections who allowed it in their own privacy settings.

Is my professional data uploaded?

No. The CSV is parsed in your browser and never leaves your device.
