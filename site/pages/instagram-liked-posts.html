FAQ

Likes & comments, found.

How do I see all the posts I liked on Instagram?

The app’s “Your activity → Likes” shows recent likes only. Your data download lists every like Instagram keeps, with the account and date. Drop it here and open the Likes & comments finder.

Can I see every comment I’ve written?

Yes — the download includes your comments with the account they were on and the date. Search any word you remember.

Why does a post no longer open?

If the owner deleted it, archived it or made their account private, Instagram won’t show it anymore — even though it’s still listed in your data.

Is this private?

Yes. Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Nobody is notified.
