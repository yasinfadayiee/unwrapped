Understand your Telegram chat statistics

Message shares, reply times, streaks, words and emojis: what your Telegram JSON export can show, privately in your browser.

The short answer

Telegram has no built-in chat statistics. Export your chat as JSON with Telegram Desktop, then drop result.json on the Telegram chat analyzer. A single chat or a whole-account JSON export works.

Who texts most?

In Chat overview, Who talks more compares message share per person. For groups, use Person by person to check each participant’s message count and share; the overview bar shows up to five people.

Who replies faster and starts conversations?

The overview highlights who replies fastest using typical reply time. It also shows who starts most conversations after a quiet spell of 6+ hours. These describe messaging habits, not anyone’s feelings.

When do you talk?

By hour of day and By weekday show your daily and weekly patterns. Messages per month appears when the export spans more than one month. The overview also highlights your busiest day, peak hour and busiest weekday, plus night-owl activity between midnight and 5 am when present.

Days talked and streaks

Days you talked counts days with messages; Longest streak shows the longest run of consecutive active days. Messages is the total and Per day is messages per day across the chat’s date span.

Words, emojis and each person’s habits

Most used words and Favourite emojis show recurring vocabulary and emojis. Person by person adds words per message, typical reply time, media sent, links shared, deleted messages and top emojis for each person, based on what the export contains; it does not recover deleted text.

Groups, cards and access

Message shares and Person by person help compare group participants; Who cares more? is available only for chats with exactly two people. Chat Wrapped makes a shareable card. For an export containing several chats, look for All chats.

The free view includes the overview, top 5 words and emojis, and the first 2 people; Plus unlocks rankings of up to 20 words and 12 emojis, every person, cards without the watermark and all chats.

Your file is read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, not even names, and nobody in the chat is notified.

FAQ

Who texts most in a Telegram group?

Compare message shares in Who talks more, then check individual counts in Person by person; Plus unlocks every person.

Why is my HTML export rejected?

The analyzer accepts Telegram Desktop JSON only. Export again as JSON and choose result.json.

Can I analyze a whole-account export?

Yes. With several chats in the JSON file, All chats lets you browse them; access to all chats requires Plus.

Does the other person get notified?

No. Analysis happens in your browser and sends no notification.

Is it free?

The overview, top 5 words and emojis, and first 2 people are free. Plus unlocks the full rankings, every person, clean cards and all chats.
