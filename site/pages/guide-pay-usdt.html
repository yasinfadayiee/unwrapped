Pay with USDT, step by step.

Never paid with crypto before? It takes about ten minutes the first time. Here’s exactly what to tap — from an exchange or from a wallet app.

What you need

  • USDT (a dollar-pegged “stablecoin”: 1 USDT ≈ 1 US dollar).
  • On the TRON (TRC20) network — the same USDT on another network will not arrive.
  • About 1–2 USDT extra to cover the withdrawal or network fee.

Option A: from an exchange (Binance, OKX, Bybit…)

  1. Buy USDT with a card or bank transfer in the exchange app (availability depends on your country).
  2. In Unwrapped, choose your plan and tap Continue to payment. Keep that screen open.
  3. In the exchange, open Wallet → Withdraw (or “Send”), choose USDT and paste our address.
  4. For Network, pick TRON (TRC20) — it may be shown as “TRX” or “Tron”.
  5. Enter the amount so that the amount received is exactly the amount on your order screen. The exchange subtracts its fee, so add it on top.
  6. Confirm the withdrawal (exchanges often ask for an e-mail or 2FA code).

Option B: from a wallet app (Trust Wallet, TronLink)

  1. Buy USDT on TRON in the app (in Trust Wallet it’s listed as “USDT · TRC20” or “Tether USD (Tron)”).
  2. Keep a little TRX in the same wallet: sending USDT from your own wallet costs a small TRON network fee paid in TRX.
  3. On the payment screen, tap Open in Trust Wallet — the address and amount are filled in. Or scan the QR code.
  4. Check the amount and confirm.

After you pay

  • Your plan unlocks automatically about a minute after the payment arrives, and you get a license key. Save it — it works on any device.
  • Not unlocked after a few minutes? Open “Already paid? Paste your transaction hash” and paste the TxID from your exchange or wallet.
  • Order timer ran out? A payment that arrives in the next hours is still accepted.
  • Still stuck? Contact us with your order ID — the links are on the payment screen.

Common mistakes

  • Wrong network. Sending on Ethereum (ERC20), BNB Chain (BEP20) or another network loses the funds. Always TRON (TRC20).
  • Fee taken from the amount. If less than the order amount arrives, you’ll be asked to send the difference.
  • No TRX in a personal wallet. Without TRX for the network fee, the transfer fails.

FAQ

Why do you only accept crypto?

It lets us sell worldwide without accounts or storing card details. You never give us your name or card number.

Do I need to create an account with Unwrapped?

No. You get a license key after paying. An e-mail is optional, for your key and a renewal reminder.

Does the plan renew automatically?

No. Crypto payments can’t be pulled from your wallet, so nothing renews unless you pay again.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The free version and the live demo show what every tool does before you buy.
