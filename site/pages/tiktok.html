TikTok FAQ

TikTok data, explained.

How do I see how much time I spend on TikTok?

Request your TikTok data (Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data, choose “All data” and JSON), then drop the ZIP here. Unwrapped reconstructs your viewing sessions from the watch history and totals the time.

Can I find a TikTok video I watched before?

Yes. Open Video finder: every video you watched, liked, saved or shared is listed with its date and time. Pick the day and time of day you remember and open the video again. The export has no titles, so the date and time are your best clues.

Is there an official TikTok Wrapped?

TikTok doesn’t offer a full Wrapped of your viewing habits. Unwrapped builds one from your own data export — hours, binges, peak hour and persona — as a story-sized card you can share.

Why is my watch history missing?

Make sure you requested All data. Custom selections may leave the watch history out. Exports also only include the history TikTok still keeps.

How accurate is the time estimate?

The export records when each video started, not how long you watched it. We treat views less than 10 minutes apart as one session, measure from the first to the last view and add a short tail for the final video. It’s an estimate — but a consistent, honest one.

Do you need my TikTok login?

Never. Everything is computed from your downloaded file, inside your browser.
