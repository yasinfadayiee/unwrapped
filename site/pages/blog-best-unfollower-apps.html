Best Instagram unfollower apps (2026).

Most “unfollower apps” want your Instagram password — the fastest way to a locked account. Here’s how the main options compare, and which ones are actually safe.

There are three kinds of tools: apps that log in as you, browser extensions that use your open Instagram session, and tools that read the official data export Instagram gives you. Only the last kind never touches your account.

How we compared them

  • Account safety — does it need your password or act on your account?
  • Privacy — is your data uploaded to someone’s server?
  • Unfollower history — can it show who unfollowed you, not just who doesn’t follow back?
  • Extras — mutuals, bot detection, other platforms.

1. Unwrapped

Unwrapped reads the ZIP from Instagram’s “Download your information” directly in your browser: who doesn’t follow back, mutuals, fans, bot followers, and who unfollowed you by comparing exports. It also covers TikTok screen time, X, Facebook, Threads and LinkedIn. The analysis is free; paid plans add full lists, CSV export and history. Trade-off: no real-time alerts — you re-download your data every week or two.

2. Unfollr and ListDiff

Both also work from the official export without a login, which makes them safe choices too. Unfollr focuses on Instagram follower tracking; ListDiff is a general list-comparison site with guides for Instagram and Threads. Check each site’s privacy policy for how files are handled.

3. Browser extensions & scripts

Extensions and console scripts read your follower lists through the Instagram tab you’re logged in to. They don’t need your password, but they act through your session, so heavy use can trigger rate limits. Install only from sources you trust — an extension can read everything on the page.

4. Login-based tracker apps

App-store trackers that ask you to sign in with Instagram can show near-live changes, but they act as you. That breaks Instagram’s terms, often triggers “action blocked”, and puts your password in a third party’s hands. See are unfollower apps safe?

Our verdict

If you care about your account, pick an export-based tool. For the widest coverage — several platforms, bot detection, mutuals and unfollower history in one place, without uploading anything — try Unwrapped or the live demo first.

FAQ

What is the safest app to see who unfollowed me on Instagram?

Tools that read your official data export — such as Unwrapped, Unfollr or ListDiff — because they never need your password or act on your account.

Is there a free Instagram unfollower app?

Yes. Export-based tools like Unwrapped show who doesn’t follow you back for free.

Do unfollower apps get you banned?

Apps that log in as you and automate actions can get your account blocked. Export-based tools can’t, because they never touch your account.
