Who viewed my Instagram profile?

Short answer: nobody can tell you — not Instagram, not any app. Here’s why, what the “profile viewer” apps really do, and what you can actually find out.

Instagram doesn’t record it for you

Instagram does not show profile visitors, and it isn’t in the data file Instagram gives you either. If it isn’t in your own export, no outside app can know it.

What “profile viewer” apps really do

Apps that promise a list of visitors usually guess from likes and comments — or invent names. To “scan” your account they ask for your password, which can get you action-blocked, hacked or banned. Read more in are unfollower apps safe?

What you can see — for real

  • Story viewers: open your story within 48 hours and swipe up. That list is real.
  • Who interacts with you most: your export holds every like, comment and message, so you can see who is really paying attention.
  • Who doesn’t follow you back and who quietly unfollowed you.
  • Fake and inactive followers with the fake follower checker.

FAQ

Can I see who viewed my Instagram profile?

No. Instagram doesn’t share profile visitors with anyone, and they aren’t in your data download.

Are profile viewer apps safe?

Most aren’t. They need your password or guess the results. Giving your login to a third party can get your account restricted.

Can people see that I viewed their profile?

No. Only story views and live views are visible to the other person.
