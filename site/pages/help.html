Help center

How can we help?

Quick fixes for the problems people run into most. Still stuck? The contact is at the bottom of this page.

Uploading your file

The upload button does nothing on my phone

You’re probably inside the Instagram, TikTok or Facebook browser — they often can’t pick files. Tap ⋯ and choose Open in browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android), then try again.

Where is the ZIP on my iPhone?

Downloads go to the Files app → Downloads. Tap “Choose my export” and pick the ZIP from there. Don’t unzip it first.

“We couldn’t find social media data”

The most common reasons: the download contains only photos and videos, it was requested as PDF, or single files were picked instead of the whole ZIP. Request the data again in JSON format and drop the ZIP exactly as you received it. The message on screen tells you which case it is.

My export came in several ZIP parts

Select all parts at once (hold Ctrl or Shift), or put them in one folder and drop the folder. They’re read together.

Is it safe to drop my export here?

Yes. The file is read inside your browser and never uploaded. You can turn on airplane mode after the page loads — it still works.

Downloading your data

My Instagram download hasn’t arrived

Small requests are often ready in minutes, large ones can take hours or a few days. Check your e-mail and Accounts Center → Download your information. The link expires after a few days — if it did, request it again.

Which options should I choose?

For unfollowers: Followers and following, format JSON, date range All time. For messages, stats and privacy: All available information. See the step-by-step guide.

How do I download TikTok, X, Facebook or LinkedIn data?

Each platform page has the exact steps: TikTok, X, Facebook, LinkedIn.

Results

“Who unfollowed you” is empty

It needs two exports from different dates. Your first one is saved as a snapshot; drop a fresh export a week or two later and the comparison appears. Still have an older export on your computer? Select it together with the new one and you’ll see the comparison right away.

My numbers differ slightly from the app

Exports can lag a few hours behind the app and leave out deactivated accounts. Small differences are normal.

My history disappeared

Snapshots live in your browser. Clearing site data, private windows or a different browser start fresh. Use Download backup in “Who unfollowed you” to keep a copy, and Restore backup to bring it back.

Payments & licenses

I sent a slightly different USDT amount

Small exchange fees are tolerated. If too little arrived, the payment page shows the missing amount: send it to the same address and paste the new transaction hash.

I sent USDT on the wrong network

Only TRON (TRC20) payments reach the order. Funds sent on Ethereum, BNB Chain or other networks can’t be detected automatically — contact us with the transaction hash.

I lost my license key

If you entered an e-mail at checkout, the key is in that e-mail. On the device you bought on, My license shows it too. Otherwise contact us with your transaction hash.

Does my plan renew automatically?

Never. Plans are one-time payments; you get a reminder before a time-limited plan ends and decide yourself whether to extend.
