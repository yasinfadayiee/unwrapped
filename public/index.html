04 — How it works

Three minutes of your time. Zero risk.

Every major platform lets you download a copy of your own data — in many countries it’s your legal right. Here’s exactly where to tap.

Request your export

  1. In the Instagram app: your profile → ☰ menu → Accounts Center.
  2. Your information and permissions → Download your information → Download or transfer information.
  3. Pick your Instagram account → Some of your information → tick Followers and following.
Open the download page

Choose these options

  1. Download to device · date range All time.
  2. Format JSON (HTML works too) · media quality Low.
  3. Want DM stats and the full privacy X-ray? Choose All available information instead.

Drop the ZIP here

Instagram e-mails you when the file is ready — usually within minutes, sometimes up to 48 hours. Download the ZIP and drop it on this page. That’s it.

I have my ZIP

Request your data

  1. In the TikTok app: profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Account.
  2. Tap Download your data.
  3. On a computer? Use the web page below.
Open TikTok’s page

Pick “All data” + JSON

  1. Choose All data — your watch history is only included there.
  2. File format JSON (TXT also works).
  3. Tap Request data.

Download & drop

TikTok can take a few hours to a few days. When it’s ready, open the Download data tab, save the ZIP and drop it here.

Request your archive

  1. On X: More → Settings and privacy → Your account.
  2. Download an archive of your data.
  3. Confirm with your password on X (never here) and the code X sends you.
Open X’s page

Wait for the notification

  1. X prepares the archive — usually within about 24 hours.
  2. You’ll get a notification and an e-mail when it’s ready.

Download & drop

Download the ZIP from the same settings page and drop it here. Huge archive? No problem — we only read the small data files, never your media.

07 — Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is it really private?

Yes. Your export is opened and analyzed entirely inside your browser — it is never uploaded, and our servers never see it. Check it yourself: open your browser’s developer tools (Network tab) or turn on airplane mode after the page loads. The analysis still works.

The only time this site talks to its own server is for licenses and payments — never with your social data.

Why don’t you ask for my password like other apps?

Because giving your password to a third-party app is the most common way accounts get hacked, restricted or banned — and the platforms forbid it. The official data export contains the same information, with none of that risk.

Can one export show who unfollowed me?

One export is a snapshot: it shows who doesn’t follow you back, your fans, pending requests and much more. Drop a newer export later and Unwrapped compares the two automatically — that’s how you see exactly who unfollowed you, by name.

How long does the export take?

Instagram and Facebook: usually minutes, at most about 48 hours. TikTok: a few hours to a few days. X: around 24 hours. LinkedIn: about 10 minutes for connections.

Which file do I upload?

The ZIP the platform sends you, exactly as it is. Split into several ZIP files? Select them all at once. Already unzipped? Choose the folder. JSON, HTML and TikTok’s TXT format all work.

Will this get my account banned?

No — we never touch your account. You download your own data with the platform’s official tool. The unfollow queue only opens profiles for you; you tap unfollow yourself, at a pace platforms are comfortable with.

Which plan should I choose?

Plus if you want the complete picture of your own account: every list in full, the names of who unfollowed you, search and CSV. Pro if you want to understand your relationships — the Ghost Detector, Engagement Audit, Growth Time Machine, Friendship Radar and the unfollow queue. Creator if your account is your work: bot detection, the best time to post and white-label reports for clients.

Not sure? Open the demo — every tool is unlocked there.

Can I upgrade later without losing what I paid?

Yes. When you move to a higher tier, the time you have left is converted at its fair value and added on top — for example, 100 days of Plus become about 59 days of Pro. Your license key stays the same. Lifetime Pro owners can add Creator time and fall back to lifetime Pro when it ends.

How do crypto payments work?

Choose a plan and pay with USDT on the TRON network — or with 150+ other coins when available. Your payment is detected on the blockchain and your plan activates automatically, usually within a minute. You get a license key that works on any device.

I don’t own crypto. Can I still buy?

Most wallet apps and exchanges let you buy USDT with a bank card in a few minutes, then send it to the address shown at checkout. Make sure you choose the TRON (TRC20) network.

What do I get for free?

Every platform, every overview and chart, the first 20 rows of every list, how many people unfollowed you, the headline numbers of every Pro Lab tool and Wrapped cards with a small watermark. Paid plans reveal the names and details behind those numbers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Download the ZIP on your phone, open this site and tap “Choose my export”. It works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android.

What is stored, and where?

Only snapshots of follower lists, inside your own browser (IndexedDB), so we can tell you who unfollowed you next time. Delete them any time from My license → Local data.
