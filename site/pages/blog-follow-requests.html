Every follow request you ever sent.

Requests to private accounts that never answered can sit pending for years, and the other person can still see them in their requests. Here’s how to list every one and cancel them.

The short answer

Instagram’s Help Center doesn’t describe a list of the follow requests you sent, but your data download has one. The file connections/followers_and_following/pending_follow_requests.json lists every request that is still waiting, with the date you sent it. Drop the ZIP on Unwrapped and you get that list, with a button to open each account.

Where to find them

Export your information from Instagram with Followers and following selected, in JSON. In the ZIP, pending_follow_requests.json holds the requests you sent, and follow_requests_you’ve_received.json holds the ones waiting for you to approve.

See and cancel them in minutes

  1. Download your Instagram data with Followers and following ticked, format JSON.
  2. Drop the ZIP into Unwrapped and open Follow requests. You’ll also see requests you received.
  3. Open each account and tap Requested to cancel — or use the paced cancel queue so you never hit Instagram’s limits.

Everything is read on your device. Unwrapped never logs in to your account and never cancels anything for you.

FAQ

Can I see follow requests I sent on Instagram?

Not as a list in the app — but your Instagram data download includes all pending requests you sent.

Do Instagram follow requests expire?

Instagram’s Help Center doesn’t mention an expiry. A request stays pending until the person accepts or declines it, or you cancel it. That’s why very old requests still show up in your download.

Why did my follow request disappear?

Usually because the person accepted it, declined it, or you cancelled it. It also disappears if the account was deleted or deactivated, or if they blocked you.

Does the person know when I cancel?

No notification is sent. The request simply disappears from their list.

Is it safe to cancel many at once?

Go slowly. Cancelling hundreds in minutes can trigger a temporary block, which is why the cancel queue spaces them out.
