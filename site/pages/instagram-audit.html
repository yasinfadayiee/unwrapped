FAQ

Account audits, explained.

How do I audit my Instagram account for free?

Download your Instagram information (followers and following, plus likes and comments for the full picture), drop the ZIP on this page and open “Account health”. You get a score from 0 to 100, a grade and the factors behind it — free.

How is the health score calculated?

It combines the factors your export can measure — follow-back balance, audience authenticity, two-way engagement, active following, growth momentum and (with two exports) follower retention — weighted into one score. Factors that can’t be measured are left out rather than guessed.

Can I audit a client’s account?

Yes, with their permission: ask them to download their own data export and send you the ZIP. It’s analyzed on your device, and the Creator plan turns it into a branded PDF report.

What’s in the PDF report?

Audience at a glance, growth, audience quality, best times to post, engagement and a list of recommendations — on one page, with your brand and your notes.

Do you need the account password?

No. We never log in to any account. Everything comes from the official export.
