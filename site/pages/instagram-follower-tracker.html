FAQ

Follower tracking, explained.

How can I track who follows and unfollows me on Instagram?

Download your followers from Instagram now and again in a week or two, and drop each ZIP here. Unwrapped compares the snapshots and lists who followed and who left.

Does it track in real time?

No. We never log in to your account, so tracking works from the exports you download. Most people repeat it every one or two weeks.

Where is my history stored?

Only in your browser. You can save a backup file at any time and restore it on another device.

Is it free?

Yes — the analysis, the counts and the growth chart are free. Plus shows every name and keeps your full history.
