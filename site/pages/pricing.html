Plans & payments

Good questions, straight answers.

Which plan is right for me?

Plus gives you the complete picture of your own account. Pro adds the Pro Lab — the tools that explain your relationships. Creator is for accounts that are work: bot detection, the best time to post and branded reports for clients. Every tool is unlocked in the live demo, so you can try before you pay.

Can I upgrade later without losing what I paid?

Yes. Moving to a higher tier converts the time you have left at fair value (based on the yearly prices) and adds it on top — 100 days of Plus become about 59 days of Pro. Moving down works the same way, in your favour. Your license key never changes.

I have lifetime Pro. Can I use the Creator tools?

Yes — buy Creator time with the same key. Creator runs for the time you bought, then your license falls back to lifetime Pro automatically.

How fast is activation?

Usually under a minute after your transaction confirms. Your plan unlocks on this device automatically, and you get a license key to use on others.

Which network do I use for USDT?

TRON (TRC20) only. Send the exact amount shown at checkout — it’s unique to your order, which is how we match your payment instantly. Sending from an exchange? Make sure the amount that arrives is the exact amount (add the withdrawal fee on top).

I sent the wrong amount. What now?

Open the checkout again (or My license → pending order) and paste your transaction hash. If you sent too little, you’ll see how much is missing and can top up with a second transfer.

Is it a subscription?

No automatic charges — crypto can’t be pulled from your wallet. Monthly and yearly plans simply end; renew whenever you like and the new time is added on top.

Is there a referral program?

Yes. Every paid license gets an invite link on the My license page. Friends who buy through it get 10% off any plan, and you get 30 free days for each one — added automatically.

Can I get a refund?

Because plans activate instantly and the free tier and demo let you test everything first, payments are final. If something doesn’t work as described, contact us and we’ll make it right. See the terms.
