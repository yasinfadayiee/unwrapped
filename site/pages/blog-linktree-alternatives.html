Best free Linktree alternatives.

A link-in-bio page turns the one link Instagram and TikTok allow into a page with everything you do. Here’s how the main options compare.

What to look for

  • A real free plan — enough links for your shop, content and contact.
  • Speed — a slow page loses visitors from your bio.
  • Click analytics — which links people actually tap.
  • No account linking — a bio page never needs your Instagram password.

The options

  • Linktree — the best-known link-in-bio tool, with a free plan and paid tiers for more customization and analytics.
  • Beacons — aimed at creators who sell, with built-in store and media-kit features.
  • Carrd — simple one-page websites; flexible, but more setup than a bio page.
  • Unwrapped Bio — a free, fast page at myunwrapped.com/@you with six themes, a featured button (great for Telegram or WhatsApp), photo, socials and click analytics. Pro is included in every Unwrapped plan.

Set up a free page in a minute

  1. Open Unwrapped Bio and pick your name.
  2. Add your links, socials and a photo, choose a theme.
  3. Copy your link and paste it into your Instagram or TikTok bio.

FAQ

Is there a free Linktree alternative?

Yes — several tools have free plans. Unwrapped Bio is free with up to 5 links, six themes and view stats.

Do link in bio tools need my Instagram password?

No. You only paste your page’s link into your bio; no account connection is needed.

Can I see which links people click?

Yes. Unwrapped Bio shows total clicks for free and per-link clicks with Pro.
