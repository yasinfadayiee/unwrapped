Request your archive

  1. On X: More → Settings and privacy → Your account.
  2. Download an archive of your data.
  3. Confirm with your password on X (never here) and the code X sends you.
Open X’s page

Wait for the notification

  1. X prepares the archive — usually within about 24 hours.
  2. You’ll get a notification and an e-mail when it’s ready.

Download & drop

Download the ZIP from the same settings page and drop it here. Huge archive? No problem — we only read the small data files, never your media.
