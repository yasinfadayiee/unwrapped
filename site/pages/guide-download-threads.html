How to download your Threads data

Your Threads download contains your followers and following lists. Request the file through Accounts Center, then use it to check who doesn’t follow back without giving an app your password.

The short answer

  1. Open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Select your Threads profile and request Followers and following.
  3. Choose JSON and All time, then create the files.
  4. When the download is ready, save the ZIP and drop it on Unwrapped for Threads.

Request the download, step by step

  1. If you start in Instagram, go to your profile and tap ☰ → Accounts Center.
  2. Open Your information and permissions → Download your information → Download or transfer information.
  3. Select your Threads profile for this request.
  4. Choose Some of your information and tick Followers and following.
  5. Choose Download to device. Set Date range: All time, Format: JSON and Media quality: Low.
  6. Tap Create files. When the e-mail and notification arrive, download the ZIP.

On a computer, start with the same Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information flow, and select your Threads profile.

JSON or HTML?

JSON is machine-readable and the most reliable choice for Unwrapped. HTML is meant for reading in a browser. Choose JSON when requesting the file for analysis.

How long does it take?

Small requests are often ready in minutes; large ones can take hours and, in rare cases, a few days. You’ll get an e-mail and a notification. The download link expires after a few days, so download it soon.

What’s inside the ZIP

  • Followers: the accounts that follow your Threads profile.
  • Following: the accounts you follow on Threads.

These are the lists needed for the follower comparison. Keep the ZIP as it is: you do not need to unzip it before dropping it on Unwrapped for Threads.

What Unwrapped shows

  • Not following back: accounts in your following list that are absent from your followers list.
  • Fans: people who follow you, but whom you do not follow back.
  • Unfollowers: accounts that disappear from your followers list when you compare a later export with the earlier snapshot.

The first export becomes a private snapshot in your browser. Add a newer export later in the same browser on the same device to compare them. A single download cannot tell you who unfollowed you before that first snapshot.

Keep the file private

Your follower lists contain other people’s account names. Unwrapped reads the ZIP on your device and nothing is uploaded. It never asks for your Instagram or Threads password. Delete extra copies of the export when you no longer need them.

FAQ

Where do I request my Threads data?

In Accounts Center, open Your information and permissions → Download your information, then select your Threads profile. Request Followers and following for this check.

Should I choose JSON or HTML?

Choose JSON for analysis in Unwrapped. It is machine-readable; HTML is designed for reading in a browser.

How long does the download take?

Small requests are often ready in minutes. Larger ones can take hours and, in rare cases, a few days. Wait for the e-mail and notification, then download the file before the link expires.

Can one export show who unfollowed me?

One export lets you compare your current followers and following. To identify unfollowers, compare a later download with your earlier snapshot; it cannot reconstruct unfollows from before that snapshot.

Do I need to share my Threads password or upload the file?

No. Unwrapped reads your downloaded ZIP in your browser on your own device. It does not ask for your password or upload the export.
